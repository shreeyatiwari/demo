The process

Simple. Fast. Secure.

Everything from listing to handoff is handled right on the platform — no sketchy meetups or lost deposits.

List your item

Why BorrowBox

Built for students,
by students.

Stop overpaying for things you only need once.

A graphing calculator for one exam. A projector for one presentation. A DSLR for one project. These things cost a fortune to buy — but a fraction of that to borrow. BorrowBox exists so students can access the tools they need without breaking the bank, while letting owners earn passive income from things sitting idle.

Our story

About us

BorrowBox was born out of a frustration most students know well — spending a chunk of your allowance on a textbook you'll use for one semester, or a calculator you need for exactly one exam. There had to be a better way.

We started as a small group of school students in Delhi who were tired of either overpaying for new things or missing out because we couldn't afford them. The idea was simple: what if students could borrow from each other instead of buying new every time?

Today, BorrowBox is a peer-to-peer marketplace where students can list, rent, and buy items from verified fellow students. We handle the payments, the security deposits, and the trust layer — so you can focus on what matters: your studies.

We believe every student deserves access to the tools they need — not just the ones who can afford to buy them outright.
